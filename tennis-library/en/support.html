Tennis Racket Notes Support

Tennis Racket Notes helps you manage personal racket profiles and stringing, play, overgrip, and maintenance logs. Your library is stored on your device.

Backups and moving devices

Open My → Data Management to export a complete backup. On the new device, choose Restore from Backup. Restore Purchases restores Pro entitlement; it does not retrieve racket records.

Purchase issues

Check your network connection and the Apple Account used for the purchase, then choose Restore Purchases on My → Pro. Pending purchases unlock after approval. Do not delete app data that has not been backed up while troubleshooting.

Contact support

Include the app version, device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce. Do not send your Apple Account password, payment password, or a complete private backup. Email: pebbox.qa@gmail.com.
