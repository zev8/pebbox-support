Privacy Policy

Scope

This policy applies to the Tennis Racket Notes iOS app, including free racket records and backup/restore features, and Pro features purchased through Apple.

On-device information and purpose

Racket details, photos, stringing, use, overgrip and maintenance logs, feel tags, preferences, and reminder settings that you enter are stored in the app on your device. They are used to display your library, compare setups, show statistics, and schedule local reminders. The developer does not receive this information through the app. The app has no account, developer-hosted cloud sync, advertising, tracking, or third-party behavioral analytics SDK.

Photos and notifications

You choose photos through the system photo picker; the app does not browse your entire photo library. A smaller copy of a selected photo is made for the racket profile. The app requests notification permission only when you enable system notifications. Declining permission does not affect records or backups. Notifications may show a racket name and check reminder on the Lock Screen; you can turn off notifications or previews in Settings. Reminders use dates and durations you entered. The app does not collect location, sensor, or health data.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and payments. The app uses StoreKit transaction verification to determine Pro access. The developer does not receive your payment card, payment password, or Apple Account password. Purchase entitlements are not included in racket-library backups. Apple handles transaction and account information under its own privacy policy.

Sharing, export, and system backup

The app does not automatically upload your library to the developer. You may choose to export or share data with an app or location you select. Backups include photos, records, and recently deleted items and are not encrypted; store them only somewhere you trust. The receiving service handles data under its own policy. iOS device backups may include app data depending on your system and iCloud settings; this is not cloud sync provided by this app.

Retention and deletion

Information remains on your device until you change or delete it. In-app deletion moves items to Recently Deleted and does not immediately erase them permanently; a copy from before a full restore is also kept on the device. To remove all app data from the device, use iOS Storage settings to delete the app. Offloading the app keeps its documents and data. Exported files, shared copies, and system backups must be managed or deleted separately. Reinstalling or restoring a system backup may bring retained information back.

Your choices

You can view and edit your information in the app, export a backup, change reminders, or revoke notification permission in Settings. Backup and restore do not require Pro. Export a backup before changing devices. Restore Purchases restores purchase entitlement; it does not retrieve your racket library.
