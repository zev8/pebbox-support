Pro one-time purchase

Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. It provides additional rackets and stringing setups, stringing reminders, and setup comparisons. It does not renew automatically. The price shown by the App Store and on its purchase confirmation sheet applies. The amount of data you can store is limited by device storage and library capacity.

Purchases, restores, and refunds

Purchases are associated with your Apple Account. After reinstalling or changing devices, use Restore Purchases. Apple handles refunds under its policies; a verified refund or revocation changes entitlement. Existing records remain available and editable, and reminder settings remain saved, but Pro reminders stop being scheduled. If the app is not running, it may not immediately process a revocation or cancel already scheduled system notifications.

Information and backups

Records are entered by you for equipment management and review. Keep a complete backup in a trusted location. Restoring a full backup replaces the current library and keeps a local pre-restore copy. A newer backup format requires a compatible app version; older app versions may not read it. Purchase entitlement and racket data are restored separately.

Reminders use recorded stringing dates and play duration and may be affected by notification permission, system scheduling, and device settings.
