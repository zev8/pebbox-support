一匣小调支持

一匣小调是一款可触碰弹奏的口袋音乐盒，包含15件乐器与场景、16首内置旋律，以及本地演奏记录和个人乐谱库。

需要帮助或反馈问题，请联系：pebbox.qa@gmail.com。请写明“一匣小调 / Melotuck”、设备型号、系统版本、应用版本与操作步骤；如需截图，请先遮去私人内容。请勿发送密码、验证码、银行卡号或完整购买凭证。

怎样开始弹奏？

轻点或滑过琴键即可弹奏。右上角“…”会展开功能栏；点击空白处可再次显示入口。网格图标可切换乐器，音符列表图标可进入旋律收藏。

什么内容免费？

拇指琴的自由弹奏、内置旋律、录制与回放永久免费。其他乐器及个人乐谱库包含在完整版本中。

试用与买断有什么区别？

全App共一次连续24小时试用，主动领取后开始；全部乐器与个人乐谱库共用截止时间，切换乐器、恢复购买或重装不会重新计时。到期不扣费、无需退订。完整版本一次买断，包含当前及未来加入本App的乐器与个人乐谱库，无订阅、无逐乐器收费。购买价格以App Store确认页为准。

如何恢复购买？

在购买页底部或“设置与购买”中点击“恢复购买”，确认使用原购买的Apple账户并连接网络。恢复的是有效购买权益，不会恢复已经删除的本地演奏或乐谱。若仍有问题，请通过支持邮箱说明现象，无需提供账户密码。

试用结束后，保存的内容还在吗？

已保存演奏仍可回放。导入乐谱不会因试用到期被删除，仍可查看和删除；继续播放个人乐谱需要完整版本。拇指琴仍免费。

录制需要麦克风吗？

不需要。录制保存按键产生的音符、节奏、乐器与调性，最长三分钟，不录周围声音，也不提供音频文件导出。

可以导入哪些乐谱？

个人乐谱库支持MIDI（.mid、.midi）与UTF-8、非压缩的score-partwise MusicXML（.musicxml、.xml），文件不超过10MB，曲目不超过30分钟和50,000个音符。图片、扫描PDF和压缩MXL不能直接导入播放。可先用其他识谱软件转换为支持的文件。

为什么导入后听感和原曲不同？

应用按音符与节奏演奏，使用所选乐器的自然衰减音色，不还原人声或完整编曲。部分表情、踏板、反复和其他演奏记号可能近似处理或按书写顺序播放，导入页会显示提示。只有对应可见琴键的音高会点亮，屏幕外音高也可能发声。DJ与鼓组不用于旋律谱。

没有声音或商品加载失败怎么办？

先检查设备音量及蓝牙/耳机输出，再使用应用提示的重试操作。商品加载与恢复需要网络和App Store；可重新加载商品，或稍后重试。已可用的免费乐器、保存的演奏与本地内容不需要重新下载。

文件保存在哪里？

演奏、乐谱和偏好由应用保存在本机，应用不向开发者上传这些内容。卸载可能丢失本地文件，请保留原始乐谱；目前没有应用自建云同步。系统备份、设备迁移及iCloud Drive文件选择遵循你自己的系统设置。

Melotuck support

Melotuck is a playable pocket music box with 15 instruments/scenes, 16 built-in melodies, local performance recordings and a personal score library.

Contact pebbox.qa@gmail.com. Include “Melotuck”, your device, OS and app version, and steps to reproduce. Remove private content from screenshots. Never send passwords, verification codes, payment-card details or full purchase receipts.

Getting started

Tap or sweep across the keys. The ellipsis at the upper right reveals the controls; tap an empty area to show it again. The grid icon opens the instrument collection, and the music-list icon opens Melody collection.

Free access, trial and full version

Kalimba free play, built-in melodies, recording and replay are always free. Claim one continuous 24-hour trial for all instruments and the personal score library. All content shares the same trial end time. Switching instruments, reinstalling or restoring does not restart it. No automatic charge or cancellation is required. A single purchase unlocks the full version, including future instruments added to this app. No subscription or per-instrument charge. Prices follow the App Store confirmation screen.

Restoring purchases

Use Restore purchases at the bottom of the purchase page or in Settings and purchases. Connect to the network and use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Restoration checks valid access; it does not recover deleted local performances or score files. Contact support if the issue continues, without sending account credentials.

After the trial ends

Saved performances remain replayable. Imported scores are kept and can be viewed and deleted; further personal score playback requires full-version access. Kalimba remains free.

Recording

Recording saves key-generated notes, rhythm, instrument and key for up to three minutes. It does not use the microphone or record ambient sound. Audio-file export is not included.

Importing scores

Supports MIDI (.mid/.midi) and uncompressed UTF-8 score-partwise MusicXML (.musicxml/.xml), up to 10 MB, 30 minutes and 50,000 notes. Images, scanned PDFs and compressed MXL do not play directly. Convert them using other score-recognition software first.

Why playback sounds different

Scores play as notes and rhythm using the chosen instrument and its natural decay. Vocals and full source arrangements are not reproduced. Some expression, pedal, repeat and other markings are approximated or play in written order; review the import notes. Only pitches corresponding to visible keys light up, but other pitches can still sound. DJ and drums are not used for melody scores.

Audio or store problems

Check device volume and headphone or Bluetooth output, then use the retry option shown by the app. Product loading and restoration require the App Store and a network connection. Reload products or try later. Available local content does not need to be downloaded again.

Local files

Performances, scores and preferences are stored on this device and are not uploaded to the developer by the app. Deleting the app may remove local files, so keep your original scores. The app does not provide its own cloud sync. System backup, device migration and files selected from iCloud Drive follow your system settings.
