一匣小调隐私说明

本说明适用于一匣小调（英文名称Melotuck，Bundle ID：studio.zev.KalimbaDuo），说明当前应用在弹奏、保存、文件导入和购买过程中如何处理数据。

账户、广告与追踪

应用无需注册账户，不含广告或第三方分析SDK，不通过应用追踪你，也不请求麦克风权限。没有运行时AI服务或开发者运营的用户内容上传服务。

本地演奏、乐谱与偏好

按键录制保存音符、时序、乐器和调性；导入乐谱保存曲名、解析后的音符及相关信息。上述内容与偏好保存在设备上，应用不会把它们上传给开发者。录制不是麦克风录音。

文件访问

仅在你主动选择导入文件时，应用读取所选MIDI或MusicXML并在本地解析，不把文件送往第三方识谱服务。文件来源可能由你选择的系统文件提供商提供，该服务的数据处理由其自身政策和你的系统设置决定。

购买、恢复与试用

付款与购买历史由Apple App Store和StoreKit处理。应用在设备上验证Apple签名的交易信息，用于检查完整版本权益、开始或恢复一次全App24小时试用，以及处理撤销情况。开发者不通过应用接收银行卡信息。购买和恢复需要连接Apple服务。

本机计时与钥匙串

应用使用本机时间、系统运行时间及设备钥匙串中的时间校准值，避免通过回拨时钟延长试用。这不是广告标识或跨应用追踪。重新安装不会重新获得已领取的试用。

保留、删除与系统备份

你可以在应用内删除个人乐谱。演奏记录与乐谱仅由本机应用保存，卸载可能丢失；恢复购买不会恢复已删除文件。请保留原始乐谱。系统备份、设备迁移、钥匙串保留及Apple服务遵循系统行为与设置，不应将应用无自建云同步理解为系统绝不备份数据。

支持邮件

如你主动发送邮件，邮件地址、正文及自愿提供的截图或设备信息会用于处理该问题。请不要附密码、验证码、支付卡资料或不必要的个人内容。删除或更正支持邮件信息的请求可发至下列支持邮箱。

Apple与其他系统服务

App Store交易、系统诊断或你启用的系统服务由Apple及相应服务提供商按其政策处理。这里关于“应用不向开发者上传”的说明，不代表Apple或你主动使用的文件/邮件服务不处理任何信息。

变更与联系

当实际数据处理发生变化时，本说明会随之更新。问题、隐私请求与反馈请联系 pebbox.qa@gmail.com。

Melotuck privacy notice

This notice applies to Melotuck (Chinese name 一匣小调; bundle identifier studio.zev.KalimbaDuo) and explains data handling during playing, saving, score import and purchases.

Accounts, advertising and tracking

No account is required. The app does not include ads or third-party analytics SDKs, track you through the app, or request microphone access. There is no runtime AI service or developer-operated user-content upload service.

Local performances, scores and preferences

Key recordings store note events, timing, instrument and key. Imported scores store their title, parsed notes and related information. These files and preferences are stored on your device; the app does not upload them to the developer. Recording does not capture microphone audio.

Selected files

The app reads a MIDI or MusicXML file only when you choose it for import and parses it locally. Files are not sent to a third-party score-recognition service. Files may come from a system file provider you choose; that provider handles data under its own policies and your system settings.

Purchases, restoration and trial

Apple App Store and StoreKit process payments and purchase history. The app verifies Apple-signed transaction information on your device to check full-version access, begin or restore the single 24-hour app trial, and handle revocations. The app does not send payment-card information to the developer. Purchase and restoration require Apple services.

Device timing and Keychain

The app uses device time, system uptime and a time reference in the device Keychain to prevent extending the trial by turning back the clock. This is not an advertising identifier or cross-app tracking. Reinstalling does not create another claimed trial.

Retention, deletion and system backup

You can delete personal scores within the app. Performances and scores are stored locally and may be lost if the app is deleted; Restore purchases does not recover deleted files. Keep your original scores. System backup, device migration, Keychain retention and Apple services follow system behavior and settings. The absence of app-operated cloud sync does not mean the operating system never backs up data.

Support email

If you choose to contact support, your email address, message and voluntarily included screenshots or device details are used to handle the issue. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment-card details or unnecessary personal information. Requests to delete or correct information in support messages can be sent to the address below.

Apple and other system services

Apple and relevant providers handle App Store transactions, system diagnostics and system services you enable under their own policies. Statements that the app does not upload files to the developer do not mean Apple, or a file or email service you choose, processes no information.

Changes and contact

This notice will be updated if actual data handling changes. For questions, privacy requests or feedback, contact pebbox.qa@gmail.com.
