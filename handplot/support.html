HANDPLOT · Home garden planner and journal

Help and support

Updated October 29, 2026

Handplot helps you remember what is growing, where it was planted and what you did along the way.

Start your garden

On Farm, add your first plot and choose a growing-space type. On Plots, sketch its approximate shape with tiles and mark planting positions. The layout is an illustration for your own records, not a surveyed map or acreage measurement.

Keep a record

In Records, tap + and choose watering, weeding, feeding, pruning, transplanting, observation or harvest. A photo is optional. Use the calendar and available filters to find past entries.

Handplot Pro

The free app includes one new plot, planting and care records, record photos, reminders and backup/restore. Pro is an optional one-time purchase for additional plots, plot-based record filtering, side-by-side photo comparison and farm cover customization. The purchase screen displays the price provided by Apple. There is no subscription or automatic renewal.

Restore a purchase

Use Restore Purchases on the Pro screen or in Settings, with the Apple account used for purchase. If Apple services are unavailable, retry after checking connectivity. Restore Purchases does not restore garden data.

Protect your garden data

Create a backup in Settings and keep it outside the app before changing phones or removing the app. Records and selected photos are stored locally; there is no Handplot cloud sync. Review the restore preview before confirming a replacement.

Photos, notifications and language

Camera and notification access are optional and can be changed in iOS Settings. English and Simplified Chinese can be selected in Handplot Settings.
