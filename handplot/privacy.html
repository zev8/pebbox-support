HANDPLOT · Home garden planner and journal

Privacy policy

Updated October 29, 2026

This policy describes Handplot for iOS. Handplot is a personal home-garden planner and record keeper. It does not require a Handplot account and has no developer-operated cloud synchronization, advertising or analytics service.

Garden information and photos

Farm details, plots, tile layouts, planting positions, care records, observations, harvests, chosen photos and app preferences are stored on your device. These records are not sent to the developer. Removing the app may remove this local information. Device backups and any copies you export are managed separately by the services and destinations you choose.

Photos and camera

You can choose a photo through the system photo picker or take a picture when adding a record. Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a picture. Chosen images are processed and resized locally; they are not full-resolution archives of your original photographs. Photos attached to plants and records are available without Pro. Changing the farm cover image requires Pro.

Reminders

Local reminders are optional. When you enable them, Handplot requests notification permission and schedules notifications on this device. You can change this permission in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Apple StoreKit handles the optional non-consumable Handplot Pro purchase and Restore Purchases. The app reads verified transactions to determine access. Apple processes payment credentials; Handplot does not receive your card details. Pro is a one-time purchase and does not automatically renew. Restoring a purchase restores access, not garden records.

Exports and backups

Backup and restore are initiated by you. Exported files include the garden data and selected photos contained in that backup. You choose the file destination or receiving app. A cloud drive or other recipient may process the files under its own policies. Restoring a backup replaces the current garden after you confirm the preview. Keep a separate copy before replacing data.

Your choices and contact

You can manage records within the app, export backups, and change permissions in iOS Settings. Handplot does not create a server account to delete. If you email the developer, the email provider and developer receive what you choose to send. Do not send private garden photographs, location details or payment credentials unless necessary for your support request.

Children and updates

Handplot is not designed to collect children’s personal information. We will update this policy when the app’s information handling changes.
