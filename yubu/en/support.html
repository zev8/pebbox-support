Help & Support

Yubu · iOS 1.0 · Updated October 23, 2026

Yubu is a badminton footwork training tool. For help, email pebbox.qa@gmail.com. Include the app version, device model, a description of the problem and steps to reproduce it. Do not send passwords or payment information.

Start training

Choose a built-in plan or create your own. Set the number of cues, sets and cue interval, then start your session. Place your device where you can see it safely, warm up first and begin at a comfortable pace. Yubu provides direction cues; it does not detect your movements.

Free features and Yubu Pro

The free version includes five built-in plans, one custom plan, fixed cue order and basic timing, footwork lessons, training history, training card sharing, and JSON backup and restore. Pro unlocks shuffled cue order, random timing, split-step preparation cues, custom cue duration, unlimited custom plans, weekly reminders, and plan file sharing and import. Annual and lifetime purchases provide the same Pro features.

Purchases, restoration and subscriptions

The annual option is an automatically renewing one-year subscription. Lifetime is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. The App Store shows the applicable price and currency. Use Restore Purchases in the Pro panel with the same Apple Account to restore valid purchases. Use Manage Subscriptions to view your subscription or cancel renewal. Buying lifetime access does not automatically cancel an existing annual subscription or credit its remaining value. If you cancel renewal, annual access continues until the end of the paid period. When Pro expires, your plans and records remain, and paid settings are retained; free training uses the basic settings.

Reminders and data

Weekly reminders require notification permission. The app schedules up to 60 reminders; each scheduled training day for each plan counts as one. Plans and records are stored on your device. Yubu has no app account or developer-operated cloud sync. Export a JSON backup before changing devices. Purchase restoration and training data restoration are separate processes. System backups may also include app data, depending on your settings.

If the feedback email does not open

If no mail app is configured, send a message to pebbox.qa@gmail.com using your preferred email service. Yubu does not automatically send your feedback or training data.
