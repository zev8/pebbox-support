Privacy Policy

Yubu · iOS 1.0 · Updated October 23, 2026

This policy applies to the Yubu iOS app, a badminton footwork training tool provided by WU ZHENFENG.

Data stored on your device

Training plans, training settings, session records, notes, lesson progress, language, interface preferences and the rotation state of original training tips are stored locally on your device. Yubu has no app accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs or developer-operated cloud sync. It does not upload training data to a developer server, sell data or track you across apps.

Permissions and files

The app requests permission to add images to your photo library only when you choose to save a training card. It does not read your photo library. Import and export use the system file picker and access only files you select. Weekly training reminders are optional and use local notifications on your device after you grant notification permission. You can disable plan reminders in the app or disable notifications in system settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes in-app purchases. Yubu uses StoreKit to verify transactions and subscription status and provide Pro access. The developer does not receive payment card information. Training backups do not contain purchase credentials that can restore paid access. Purchases, restoration and subscription management communicate with Apple services and are subject to Apple’s applicable policies.

Sharing, support and external links

When you choose to share, export or back up data, it is passed to the destination you select in the system interface. Cloud storage providers and other apps process files under their own policies. The feedback feature opens an email draft addressed to pebbox.qa@gmail.com for you to review and send. The developer receives only the contents and email contact information you voluntarily send, and uses them to respond to and handle support requests. Do not send passwords, complete training backups or payment information. External learning references and links such as Apple’s licence agreement open the relevant websites, which process network request information under their own policies.

Backups and deletion

You can export a JSON backup and import it into a compatible version of Yubu. System backups may include app data, depending on the system and your settings. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device but does not delete exported files or existing system backups. Manage those copies where they are stored.

Health and training

Yubu does not access HealthKit, location, the camera, microphone or contacts. Direction cues and session records are app guidance logs. They do not measure movement quality, actual footsteps, reaction speed or training results. Warm up and adjust the intensity to your circumstances. Stop if you feel pain or discomfort.

Contact and changes

For privacy or support questions, email pebbox.qa@gmail.com. We will update this policy and its date if our data practices change.
